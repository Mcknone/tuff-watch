How it works

  1. Enter coordinates (or load an example).
  2. Pick a time period and phenomena.
  3. Press Analyse. The app finds the real satellite acquisitions over your area in NASA's catalogue, downloads NASA's science imagery for each date, and measures every pixel.

Colours: each phenomenon has its own colour family; darker = more intense.
