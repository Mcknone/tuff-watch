How it works

  1. Enter coordinates or load an example.
  2. Pick a time period and phenomena.
  3. Press Analyse. EarthScope finds the real satellite acquisitions over your area in NASA’s catalogue, reads NASA’s science imagery for each date and measures every pixel.
